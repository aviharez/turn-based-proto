# Cultural sensitivity rules (binding)

Sundara is Nusantara-inspired, not a copy of any real religion or living tradition.

## Never use as enemies or comic relief
- Nyi Roro Kidul, Dewi Sri, Semar and the Punakawan, Barong and Rangda
- Any religious figure, prophet, saint, or real place of worship shown as evil
- Pocong and wewe gombel (tied to burial rites / child abduction; too upsetting)

## OK to use, with respect
- Folklore spirits as enemies: tuyul, genderuwo-like brutes, Buto Ijo (boss), Harimau Kumbang (boss), Naga Samudra (boss)
- Architecture: joglo, rumah gadang, tongkonan-inspired forms, candi ruins (as ancient, abandoned places)
- Martial arts and crafts: silat, keris, batik and songket motifs, gamelan sounds

## Rules
- Regions are fictional (Wanaraga, Ranah Tinggi, Laut Selatan). Don't name real ethnic groups as villains.
- Spirits are eerie-but-charming, never gory.
- When unsure, flag it in the PR and ask the owner.
