## Behind the scenes (AI notes)
- The first tiles looked like wallpaper: one tile repeated in a grid. It took two rounds of feedback before the AI understood "edge Wang tiles" (16 variants that hide the repeat) and that depth comes from layers (raised ground, tall props, shadows, a leafy frame), not from the floor texture. Lesson: show the AI a reference image, don't just describe it.
