**Hook (one line a stranger would stop for):** We're building a full pixel RPG in 30 days with a team of AI agents. Day 0: here's the plan.

**What changed (1–3 lines, plain words):** Picked the game: a turn-based tactics RPG set in a Nusantara-inspired archipelago, with expeditions, a village you build and a house you decorate. Set up the repo, rules for the AI agents, and a 30-day plan for Chapter 1.

**Numbers:** 3 AI agents (Claude Code, GPT, GLM) · 1 human reviewer · 30 days · Chapter 1 = 3 floors + 1 boss + a village + a house · 11 automated tests on day 0 · ~25 PixelLab + 24 SpriteCook credits spent on style tests.

**Behind the scenes / AI note:** The first tiles looked like wallpaper: one tile repeated in a grid. It took two rounds of feedback before the AI understood "edge Wang tiles" (16 variants that hide the repeat) and that depth comes from layers (raised ground, tall props, shadows, a leafy frame), not from the floor texture. Lesson: show the AI a reference image, don't just describe it.
