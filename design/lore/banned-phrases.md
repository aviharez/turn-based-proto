# Banned phrases (dialogue and UI text)

Lines containing any of these fail /text-review. Extend this list when playtesters spot new AI-isms.

- "It's not X, it's Y" (and "This isn't just X")
- "a testament to"
- "tapestry", "symphony of", "dance of" (as metaphors)
- "delve", "embark on a journey", "in the realm of"
- "little did they know"
- "unleash", "unlock your potential"
- "the air was thick with"
- "a sense of" (+ feeling)
- "only time will tell"
- "mark my words"
- "shivers down my spine"
- Ending lines with a rhetorical question to the player
- Triple adjective lists ("dark, cold and lonely")
